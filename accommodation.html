Where to stay

Accommodation

There are places to stay around Chesterfield, Baslow and the Peak District. Book early because the wedding falls on a bank-holiday weekend.

Recommended areas

Where to base yourself

Chesterfield

The widest choice of hotels, taxis and transport links. A practical option for most guests.

Roughly 10–20 minutes by car, depending on where you stay.

Baslow & Chatsworth

Inns and boutique stays closer to the Peak District, with attractive villages and countryside nearby.

Roughly 10–15 minutes by car.

Peak District cottages

A useful option for families or groups who want to stay together for the weekend.

Travel time varies, so check the route before booking.
