Where to stay

Accommodation

There are places to stay around Chesterfield, Baslow and the Peak District. We recommend booking early because the wedding falls on a bank-holiday weekend.

Recommended areas

Where to base yourself

Chesterfield

Chesterfield has the widest choice of hotels, taxis and transport links. It is likely to be the most practical option for most guests.

Approximately 10–20 minutes from the venue by car, depending on where you stay.

Baslow and Chatsworth

Baslow and the surrounding area offer country inns, boutique hotels and access to the Peak District.

Approximately 10–15 minutes from the venue by car.

Peak District cottages

A holiday cottage may work well for families or groups who would like to stay together for the weekend.

Journey times vary, so please check the route to the venue before booking.

Specific hotel recommendations, room blocks and wedding-day transport information will be added once they have been confirmed.

Getting here

Travel

The venue is The Fox & Goose, Pudding Pie Hill, Chesterfield, S42 7JJ.

The information below gives a general idea of the main driving routes. Please use live navigation before travelling because traffic, roadworks and closures may affect the journey.

From North Wales

Travel east towards the Peak District. Depending on your starting point, the route will usually use the A55 before continuing towards Greater Manchester, Buxton, Baslow and Chesterfield.

Open route

From Warwick

Travel north towards the East Midlands, generally using the M42 and M1. Leave the motorway for Chesterfield before continuing towards Pudding Pie Hill.

Open route

From Manchester

Travel southeast across the Peak District. The route will commonly pass through or near Stockport, Buxton, Bakewell or Baslow before reaching the venue.

From London

Travel north on the M1, leave the motorway for Chesterfield and then continue west towards the venue. Allow extra time for bank-holiday traffic.
