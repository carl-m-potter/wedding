Our story

How we got here

Carl and Claire first met on Claire’s birthday night out in Sheffield in 2024, where their first interaction consisted of Claire telling Carl off for wishing her a happy birthday four days too early! After seeing a Rammstein cover band, Carl returned to his home in Bracknell until their paths crossed again at the Rail Ale Festival a few weeks later.

There, Carl and Claire were able to relax and get to know each other over numerous ales, connecting over WhatsApp stickers and mead. A bond was formed, though romance wasn’t on the cards just yet.

After a couple of failed relationships, Carl and Claire maintained a close friendship underpinned by a mutual care for one another. Despite Carl holding a torch for Claire, it took the gift of a tray of flapjack for her to realise what she had in front of her. In June 2025, after a day out with Harper, she took the leap and declared her feelings for Carl.

The proposal

On a cold and drizzly day in Sheffield, Claire and Harper stood, wrapped up against the elements, eagerly waiting for Carl to complete the Sheffield Half Marathon. As he finally crossed the finish line, Claire couldn't help but think he looked surprisingly chipper for someone who had just run 13.1 miles.

Little did she know, his energy wasn't just runner's high. Carl was running on pure adrenaline and anticipation for the moment he had been planning all along.

Upon rushing over to greet Claire and Harper, Carl immediately dropped down to one knee. To Claire's absolute surprise, it wasn't from sheer exhaustion: he was asking her to marry him. It is safe to say that the O2 Academy bus shelter has never seen romance quite like it before.
