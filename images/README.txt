Replace story-1.svg, story-2.svg, story-3.svg and story-4.svg with your own image files.

The easiest method is to name your photos:
story-1.jpg
story-2.jpg
story-3.jpg
story-4.jpg

Then update the four image paths in index.html from .svg to .jpg.
Landscape photos work best. Aim for roughly 1200 x 900 pixels.
